About

Jorge E. Mendoza is a Full Professor in the Department of Logistics and Operations Management at HEC Montréal and a researcher at CIRRELT. He holds the HEC Research Professorship in Clean Transportation Analytics and serves as Scientific Advisor to IVADO Labs.

His research focuses on the design, development, and application of optimization methods in transportation science, logistics, production planning, and scheduling. He is strongly committed to open science, through open-source software for operations research and open research data. He co-founded and coordinates VRP-REP, a community platform for sharing vehicle routing benchmark instances, results, and solution checkers.

Before joining HEC Montréal in 2018, he was an associate professor (maître de conférences) at Polytech Tours and the Université Catholique de l'Ouest in France, and led the ANR-funded e-VRO research program on electric vehicle routing (2015–2019). His work received the 2022 Transportation Science Paper of the Year Award and an Honorable Mention in the 2021 INFORMS TSL Best Paper Competition.

Research interests

  • Problems: vehicle routing (electric, stochastic, dynamic), charging scheduling, arc routing, maintenance planning and scheduling
  • Methods: heuristics, metaheuristics and matheuristics, decomposition methods, learning-based optimization
  • Open science: open-source OR software and open research data
